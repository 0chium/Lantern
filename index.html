Lantern

Standalone accepted Build #51 ON header artwork on a transparent background; no slider or interface.

Warm flashlight control with a native expanded intensity slider and lantern illustration.

Unpublished preparation draft. Initially unsigned by owner decision; live Sileo/Zebra compatibility and activation/removal validation remain pending. These links are prepared, not live-validated.

Supported environment

Verified on iPhone SE (2nd generation), iOS 18.2 (22C152), Dopamine 3.0.10 rootless. The tested CCSupport version is 1.3.13-3~ios18fix1. Other environments are unverified. CCSupport is required; the release candidate declares mobilesubstrate and com.opa334.ccsupport.

Known limitations

  • Switching between Lantern and Apple's flashlight at the same brightness may require a second interaction to update the physical flashlight output.
  • Apple's expanded flashlight intensity interface can display its active indicator while Lantern is operating.

Installation, updates and removal

After this source is published and validated, refresh it in Sileo or Zebra and install Lantern. Use the package manager for updates/removal. Turn the flashlight off and close Camera before package changes.

This package has no automatic activation scripts. On the verified environment, the established root-shell activation is:

/var/jb/usr/bin/killall -TERM cameracaptured
/var/jb/usr/bin/sbreload

Both processes must restart after changes; a respring alone is not the verified complete procedure. Package-manager activation/removal validation is pending. These instructions do not claim one-tap automatic activation or compatibility beyond the tested environment.
