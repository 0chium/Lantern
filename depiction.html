Lantern 0.1.2-1

Warm flashlight control with Apple's native expanded intensity slider.

Standalone accepted Build #51 ON header artwork; no intensity bar, controls or surrounding interface.

Verified: iPhone SE 2, iOS 18.2 (22C152), Dopamine 3.0.10 rootless. Requires compatible CCSupport and mobilesubstrate. Other environments unverified.

  • Same-brightness switching may require a second interaction to update physical output.
  • Apple's expanded flashlight active indicator may appear while Lantern operates.

After normal package-manager installation, restart cameracaptured and reload SpringBoard using the documented procedure. This package has no automatic activation scripts.
